# ECHO — A Little Home for Your Thoughts 🌷

ECHO is a personal digital diary and scrapbook designed to give your thoughts, memories, feelings, and little everyday moments a place to live.

With a cozy aesthetic and a playful Neo-Brutalism-inspired design, ECHO makes journaling feel simple, personal, and enjoyable.

### ✨ Features
- **Create Memories:** Write diary entries and preserve special moments.
- **Mood Tracker:** Express how you're feeling through the "Weather Inside Me" mood selector.
- **Little Things Jar:** Keep small thoughts and moments worth remembering.
- **Things I Couldn't Say:** A space for thoughts you may not be ready to express.
- **My Memories:** Revisit, edit, favourite, and manage your saved memories.
- **Calendar:** Explore your memories by date.
- **My Space:** A personal space within ECHO.

### 💻 Platforms
- Web application
- Android application

### 🎨 Design
ECHO features a warm, cozy colour palette, scrapbook-inspired elements, playful interactions, and a Neo-Brutalism-inspired interface.
